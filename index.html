Common questions

How do I change the language?

Open Settings to switch between English and Japanese. Your choice is remembered. On first launch, Japanese is used when the device language is Japanese; otherwise, English is used.

Do I need to link an account?

Account linking is optional. Before changing devices or reinstalling the app, check the backup and transfer options in Settings. Do not erase your app data while investigating a saved-game problem.

Where is my progress saved?

Ordinary game progress is saved on your device. You can choose a cloud backup in Settings; an internet connection is required. A backup must finish successfully before you rely on it. Contact support before deleting app data if you are unsure.

A coin purchase has not appeared. What should I do?

Check your internet connection and allow any pending purchase confirmation to finish. Do not make another purchase just to retry a pending transaction. If your balance is still incorrect, contact support with your app version and a description of what happened. Do not send payment card details.

Are ads required?

Rewarded ads are optional. You can choose to watch an ad for an available in-game benefit. Advertising privacy choices are separate from usage analytics. When required, advertising privacy options are available in Settings. Consent information may be updated at app launch, before you choose to watch an ad.

Can I play without usage analytics?

Yes. You can decline usage analytics and continue playing. Change your choice in Settings. Turning analytics off does not delete your saved game.
